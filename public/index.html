Lesson Observation Rating Instrument (LORI)

Dear Assessor,

This instrument is designed to check if the teacher being observed is delivering effective lessons in the classroom. This instrument has three sections; A, B, and C. Section A is to capture details of the teacher being observed, Section B captures teacher standard, sub-category, and grading of the teacher, while section C are the observers’ summary and comments.

Thank you.

Section A: Demographic Data ▶️

LGEA School Name
Location School Code from Annual School Census
Name of teacher observed Teacher’s TRCN No.
Teacher’s gender Teacher’s phone number
Number of pupils
Teacher/Class observed Duration of Lesson Start Time: End Time:
Subject observed Years of Teaching Experience
Date Lesson is Observed Term
Age
Highest Teaching Qualification

Section B ▶️

Please assess the aspect of the lesson by placing a tick in the appropriate column on the rating scale

Rating Scale Descriptors 1: (poor); 2 (Fair) 3(Good); 4(Very Good); 5 (Excellent)

Teacher Standard Sub-category 1 2 3 4 5 Average Score Descriptor
1. Subject content knowledge a) The content is relevant
b) The content is delivered logically and sequentially
2. Planning the lesson a) The teacher prepared a lesson plan
b) The introduction was stimulating and aroused the interest and curiosity of the learners
c) The teacher referred to previous lessons and skills
3. Presentation and pedagogy a) Every learner is involved in learning and enjoying it
b) The teacher uses a variety of instructional materials to explain the concept
c) The learners use a variety of instructional materials to practice the concept
d) The learners have relevant text/workbooks
e) The learners have relevant writing materials such as pencils, biro, colouring pens, etc.
f) The teacher uses /displays audio-visual materials in the class
g) The teacher uses various ways of grouping learners
h) The teacher uses language that is relevant and understandable to the learners
i) The teacher gives clear instructions to the learners
j) New words and concepts are clearly explained and related to learners’ experiences
4. Relationship with learners a) The teacher uses learners’ names when addressing them individually
b) The teacher is fair and inclusive in their teaching and feedback
c) The teacher has empathy for the learners
d) The teacher responds to individual learners according to their need
e) The teacher is a role model to the learners
5. Class management a) Every learner can see the teacher and the board
b) The teacher praises and rewards the learners
c) The teacher encourages good behaviour among learners
d) The teacher is confident in his/her presentation
e) The teacher does not use a cane, use physical force, or threatens learners
6. Evaluation of learning a) The lesson objectives are clearly stated at the beginning of the lesson
b) The teacher walks around the room for effective teaching and learning
c) The teacher uses a variety of assessment techniques
d) The teacher invites learners to ask questions and responds appropriately
e) The teacher checks the achievement of the lesson objectives at the end of the lesson through relevant text
f) The teacher gave relevant homework if need be
7. Overall Assessment
